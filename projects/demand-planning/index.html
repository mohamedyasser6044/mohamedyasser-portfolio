Demand Planning Dashboard

A two-page Power BI report for pharmaceutical demand planning, built on Viatris Pharma supply-chain data. It answers one question for planners and commercial leadership: are we fulfilling the demand we forecast, and where is stock about to become a problem? Page one is the executive view — sales, stock position, pending orders, incoming shipments and product-level supply risk. Page two goes into demand accuracy — demand vs. actual fulfillment, forecast accuracy against target, and inventory movement month over month.

What’s in it

Business question Are we fulfilling forecast demand, how accurate was the forecast, and which products are heading for a shortage or an expiry write-off?
KPIs Current Sales & MoM % · Forecast Accuracy (FCA %) vs. target · BIAS / over- & under-selling · Demand vs. Delivered Quantity · Supply Covered % / Shortage % · Total Inventory, Warehouse (EGAB), Inbound (EGAC) & Reserve values · Pending Orders · Incoming Shipments & next ETA
Breakdowns By product · by customer · by month and year · by batch (expiry) · by warehouse delivery date
Techniques DAX measures for MoM deltas, FCA %, BIAS and Pareto cumulative % · conditional formatting driven by measure-returned colour codes · SVG measures rendering inline risk bars inside a table · page and bookmark navigators · custom branded theme
