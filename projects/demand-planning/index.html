Demand Planning Dashboard

A three-page Power BI report for demand planning in a pharma company, based on the reports I build at work at Viatris. I changed the product names and all the numbers, so nothing here is real company data. The main question it answers is simple: are we delivering what customers ordered, how good was our forecast, and which products are at risk?

Screenshots

What’s in it

Business question Are we delivering the demand we forecast, how accurate was the forecast, and which products could run short or expire?
KPIs Sales and change vs. last month · Forecast accuracy (FCA %) · Bias · Demand vs. delivered quantity · Supply covered % · Inventory, warehouse, inbound and reserve values · Pending orders · Incoming shipments
Breakdowns Product · customer · month and year · released / quarantined stock · delivery date
Techniques DAX measures for month-over-month change, FCA %, bias and Pareto % · conditional formatting with colour measures · risk bars inside a table · page navigation with bookmarks · custom theme
